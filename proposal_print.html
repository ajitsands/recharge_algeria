1. Executive Summary & Strategic Context

1.1 Market Context & Client Profile

Razik Telecom is an established B2B telecom credit distributor operating in Algiers, Algeria. The company purchases bulk wholesale mobile airtime and data packages from the three national licensed operators—Mobilis (06), Djezzy (07), and Ooredoo (05)—and distributes them to a broad network of approved retail agents including phone kiosks, cybercafés, neighborhood mini-markets, and multi-service shops.

The current operating model relies on the owner directly handling transaction approvals, credit provisioning, and physical cash collection under post-sale instalment agreements. While this has fostered close client relationships, it imposes significant operational ceilings on volume, speed, and financial control.

1.2 Current Operational Ceilings & Pain Points

ID Current Operational Bottleneck Business Risk & Operational Impact Severity
PP-01 Manual Owner Intervention Transaction delays of 2–5 minutes; inability to handle peak evening recharge surges; limits total daily revenue. CRITICAL
PP-02 Manual Operator Selection Retailers or dispatcher misidentify operator prefixes, leading to misrouted airtime and lost funds. HIGH
PP-03 Informal Post-Sale Credit & Ageing Lack of automated credit caps allows agent receivables to exceed recoverable limits without automated warning. CRITICAL
PP-04 Physical Cash Collection Discrepancies Collections performed on the road lack instantaneous numbered digital receipts, creating reconciliation friction. HIGH

1.3 Strategic Solution Vision

SaNDS Lab Middle East W.L.L has architected the Razik Telecom Platform to transition operations from manual dispatching to an automated, auditable, high-concurrency transaction switch:

  • Instant Sub-3-Second Recharges: Retailers submit recharge requests via Android APK or Web POS; the engine auto-identifies the operator via prefix, validates dealer limits, deducts the wholesale net, and executes the recharge automatically.
  • Double-Entry Ledger & Credit Control: Configurable hard/soft credit limits, automated ageing buckets, and instalment plans with automatic temporary suspension of delinquent retailers.
  • Numbered Digital Cash Collection Receipts: Field cash collectors issue digitally signed, sequential receipts allocated directly against the oldest pending receivables.
  • Widescreen 1920px Operations Center: Comprehensive real-time monitoring of wholesale balances, dealer approvals, live margins, and one-click dispute resolution.

4. Functional Scope Breakdown (The 6 Pillars)

Pillar 1: Platform Foundation, Identity & Access Control

  • Self-Service Dealer Registration: Prospective retailers submit business credentials, commercial registry number (Registre du Commerce), phone number, and store Wilaya location.
  • Owner Approval Gate: Submitted accounts remain locked in PENDING until the owner verifies identity, assigns initial credit facility, sets commission overrides, and approves activation.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict segregation of duties across Super Admin, Kiosk Dealer, Cash Collector, and Support Operator.
  • Enterprise Security: Session timeout configuration, brute force throttling, password encryption (bcrypt), and complete IP/device audit trail.

Pillar 2: High-Speed Telecom Recharge Engine

  • Automatic Prefix Detection: Analyzes Algerian phone inputs in real-time: 06 → Mobilis | 07 → Djezzy | 05 → Ooredoo.
  • Instant Commission Discount Calculation:
    Live Example: When a kiosk initiates a 100 DZD Mobilis recharge with an approved 5.00% dealer commission rate:
    • End-Customer Receives: 100 DZD airtime credit | • Dealer Wallet Debited: 95 DZD net | • Dealer Retains: 5 DZD cash profit immediately.
  • Idempotency & Duplicate Protection: Unique UUID tokens per transaction ensure that double-clicking or network drops never trigger multiple debits or duplicate recharges.
  • Controlled Pending Transaction Queue: Transactions with delayed operator acknowledgments are placed in an active monitoring queue with auto-retry and manual admin resolution controls.

Pillar 3: Receivables, Instalments & Physical Cash Collection Sub-Ledger

  • Double-Entry Ledger: Every transaction, commission, wallet replenishment, and refund generates an immutable double-entry journal entry with balance snapshots.
  • Instalment Scheduling & Ageing Analysis: Configurable payment plans with due date tracking; automatic categorization into Current, 30-Day, 60-Day, and 90-Day overdue buckets.
  • Credit Hard/Soft Stops: Automatic system warnings and conditional transaction locks when an agent exceeds their maximum credit limit or has overdue instalments.
  • Tamper-Proof Cash Collection Register: Field collectors record cash received on-site; system generates a sequential numbered receipt with automatic allocation against the oldest unpaid invoices.

Pillar 4: Operator Wholesale Quota Monitoring & Reconciliation

  • Real-Time Quota Gauges: Live visual display of Razik Telecom's remaining wholesale balances with Mobilis, Djezzy, and Ooredoo with Warning Level (20%) and Critical Lockout (5%) thresholds.
  • Daily Reconciliation: End-of-day discrepancy reporting matching operator consumption against internal ledger records.

Pillar 5: Bilingual User Experience & Multi-Channel Delivery

  • Native Arabic (RTL) & French/English (LTR): Contextual localized terminology for Algerian telecom terms (Flexy, Arsselli, Storm, Solde, Versement).
  • 1920px Widescreen Desktop Operations Center: High-density operations dashboard featuring Algerian national emblem and live Algiers digital clock (Africa/Algiers).
  • Android Mobile APK: Fast, lightweight Android application designed for kiosk counter tablets and smartphones, with support for Bluetooth thermal receipt printers.

Pillar 6: Future Foundation (Bill Utilities & E-Commerce Ready)

  • Utility Payment Connectors: Data model and workflow reservations for Sonelgaz (Electricity and Gas bill barcode settlement) and ADE (Algérienne Des Eaux water bill processing).
  • Physical Accessories E-Commerce: Pre-built database hooks for phone accessories (chargers, earphones, screen protectors) including stock tracking and delivery routing.

8. Controls, Risk Management & Mitigations

ID Identified Risk Impact Engineered Architectural Mitigation
R-01 Timeout During Recharge HIGH Idempotency Token & Managed Pending Queue: Recharges are tagged with unique UUIDs. Timeout transactions enter an active pending queue that polls operator status before releasing funds or reversing debits.
R-02 Excessive Retailer Debt HIGH Automated Credit Limits & Ageing Freeze: The system automatically blocks recharge access if an agent's balance breaches their approved credit ceiling or if instalments are overdue past agreed grace periods.
R-03 Cash Collection Discrepancy MEDIUM Sequential Unalterable Receipts: Every cash collection creates an immutable, sequentially numbered receipt sent to the dealer; errors can only be resolved via counter-signed contra entries.
R-04 Local Server Failure / Outage HIGH Automated Daily Dual-Backup: Automated cron tasks perform nightly encrypted database snapshots stored locally and synced to secure off-site cloud storage.
R-05 Ported Number Prefix Misdirection MEDIUM Manual Operator Override Toggle: While prefix auto-detection is active by default, the dealer POS interface includes a 1-click operator override if a customer has ported their line.
